About TypeMaster Pro

TypeMaster Pro is a free online typing speed test platform built to help students, professionals, programmers, writers, and anyone improve their typing speed and accuracy.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a fast, accurate, and user-friendly typing test experience that is accessible to everyone without requiring registration or payment.

What We Offer

  • Free Online Typing Speed Test
  • Real-Time WPM Calculation
  • Typing Accuracy Tracking
  • Characters Per Minute (CPM)
  • Multiple Typing Modes
  • Multiple Languages
  • Performance Analytics
  • Responsive Design
  • Dark Mode Support

Who Can Use TypeMaster Pro?

Our typing test is ideal for students, software developers, office professionals, content writers, gamers, data entry operators, and competitive exam aspirants.
