Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our free online typing speed test.

1. What is a typing speed test?

A typing speed test measures how fast and accurately you type using your keyboard. It calculates your Words Per Minute (WPM), Characters Per Minute (CPM), accuracy, and typing errors.

2. Is TypeMaster Pro completely free?

Yes. TypeMaster Pro is completely free to use. No registration or subscription is required.

3. How is WPM calculated?

WPM (Words Per Minute) is calculated by dividing the total number of characters typed by five and then dividing by the total time in minutes.

4. What is CPM?

CPM stands for Characters Per Minute. It shows the total number of characters typed in one minute.

5. Does accuracy affect my typing score?

Yes. Higher accuracy reflects better typing performance and helps you improve your overall typing skills.

6. Can beginners use this typing test?

Absolutely. Our typing test is suitable for beginners, students, professionals, and experienced typists.

7. Can I practice every day?

Yes. Daily typing practice is the best way to improve your typing speed and accuracy over time.

8. Does the website work on mobile devices?

Yes. TypeMaster Pro is fully responsive and works on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.

9. Which browsers are supported?

We support all modern browsers including Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Safari, and Opera.

10. Is my typing data stored?

Your typing statistics may be stored locally in your browser to improve your experience. We do not require an account to use the typing test.

11. Can I improve my typing speed with this website?

Yes. Regular practice with TypeMaster Pro can help increase your typing speed, accuracy, and confidence.

12. What typing modes are available?

You can practice using words, quotes, numbers, and code typing modes.

13. Does TypeMaster Pro support multiple languages?

Yes. You can practice typing in multiple supported languages.

14. What is considered a good typing speed?

Most people type between 35 and 50 WPM. Professional typists often achieve 70 WPM or higher with excellent accuracy.

15. Is registration required?

No. You can start practicing instantly without creating an account.

16. How often should I practice?

Practicing for 15–30 minutes daily can significantly improve your typing performance.

17. Is this website suitable for coding practice?

Yes. The code typing mode is useful for programmers who want to improve coding speed and keyboard efficiency.

18. Can I use TypeMaster Pro for exam preparation?

Yes. It is useful for preparing for typing tests, competitive exams, and data entry jobs.

19. Is there any limit on the number of typing tests?

No. You can take unlimited typing tests for free.

20. How can I contact TypeMaster Pro?

You can reach us through the Contact Us page for feedback, suggestions, or support.
